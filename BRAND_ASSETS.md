## 💧 Logo Concepts

### Logo Design Brief

**Icon Concept: Water Droplet + Flow**

**Primary Logo:**
```
  💧
CRMFloat
```

**Variations Needed:**
1. **Full Logo** - Icon + Text (Horizontal)
2. **Stacked Logo** - Icon above Text (Vertical)
3. **Icon Only** - Just the droplet/wave symbol
4. **Text Only** - CRMFloat wordmark
5. **Monochrome** - Black & white versions

### Icon Design Elements
- Clean, modern water droplet shape
- Subtle wave or flow lines
- Minimalist, recognizable at small sizes
- Works in single color
- Scalable vector (SVG)

### Logo Usage Guidelines

**Clear Space:**
- Minimum clear space: 0.5x logo height on all sides
- Never place text or graphics closer than clear space

**Minimum Size:**
- Digital: 24px height minimum
- Print: 0.5 inch height minimum

**Do's:**
- ✅ Use approved color combinations
- ✅ Maintain aspect ratio
- ✅ Use on solid backgrounds
- ✅ Ensure good contrast

**Don'ts:**
- ❌ Stretch or distort logo
- ❌ Change colors
- ❌ Add effects or shadows
- ❌ Place on busy backgrounds
- ❌ Rotate or flip

## 🌐 Voice & Tone

### Brand Voice

**Friendly but Professional**
- "We're here to help" not "You must do this"
- "Let's get started" not "Begin now"
- "Your customers will love this" not "This feature is required"

**Clear and Simple**
- Use plain language
- Avoid jargon
- Short sentences
- Active voice

**Empowering**
- "You can..." not "We allow you to..."
- "Take control" not "Let us manage"
- Focus on user benefits

### Writing Style

**Do:**
- ✅ Use contractions (we're, you'll, it's)
- ✅ Address users as "you"
- ✅ Be conversational
- ✅ Use bullet points
- ✅ Keep paragraphs short

**Don't:**
- ❌ Use corporate jargon
- ❌ Write long sentences
- ❌ Use passive voice
- ❌ Overcomplicate explanations
- ❌ Use ALL CAPS (except acronyms)

### Example Phrases

**Good:**
- "Float your business forward with CRMFloat"
- "Managing clients has never been this easy"
- "See all your deals flowing through your pipeline"
- "Your team will love the simplicity"

**Avoid:**
- "Leverage our synergistic platform"
- "Utilize our comprehensive CRM solution"
- "Facilitate optimal customer engagement"

## 📸 Photography & Imagery

### Image Style

**Preferred:**
- Clean, modern workspaces
- Diverse teams collaborating
- Technology in professional settings
- Bright, natural lighting
- Authentic moments (not overly staged)

**Avoid:**
- Stock photo clichés
- Dark or cluttered backgrounds
- Overly formal or stuffy images
- Low-quality or blurry photos

### Image Treatment

- **Overlay:** Subtle gradient (Float Blue to Light Cyan, 0.7 opacity)
- **Filters:** Light, bright, slightly desaturated
- **Aspect Ratios:**
  - Hero: 16:9
  - Cards: 4:3
  - Icons: 1:1

## 🎬 Animation & Motion

### Animation Principles

**Timing:**
- Fast: 0.2s (hover, small changes)
- Medium: 0.3s (cards, modals)
- Slow: 0.5s (page transitions)

**Easing:**
- Default: ease-in-out
- Entry: ease-out
- Exit: ease-in

**Effects:**
- Fade in/out
- Slide up/down
- Scale (subtle, 0.95 - 1.0)
- Transform translateY (-2px to -4px)

### Loading States

- Animated pulse on cards
- Skeleton screens (not spinners)
- Progress bars with Float Blue color
- Smooth transitions

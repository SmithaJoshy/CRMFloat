## 💰 Financial Planning

### Startup Costs Estimate

**One-Time Costs:**
- Business registration: $500-2,000
- Trademark filing: $300-1,000
- Logo/branding: $500-5,000
- Website development: $2,000-10,000
- Initial marketing: $2,000-10,000
- Legal fees: $2,000-5,000
- **Total:** $7,300-33,000

**Monthly Costs:**
- Hosting/infrastructure: $100-500
- Payment processing: 2.9% + $0.30 per transaction
- Email marketing: $50-300
- Support software: $100-500
- Monitoring tools: $50-200
- Marketing/ads: $500-5,000+
- **Total:** $800-6,500+/month

### Revenue Projections

**Conservative (Year 1):**
- Month 1-3: 10 paying customers = $290/month
- Month 4-6: 25 paying customers = $725/month
- Month 7-9: 50 paying customers = $1,450/month
- Month 10-12: 75 paying customers = $2,175/month
- **Year 1 Total:** ~$13,000

**Moderate (Year 1):**
- Month 1-3: 25 paying customers = $725/month
- Month 4-6: 75 paying customers = $2,175/month
- Month 7-9: 150 paying customers = $4,350/month
- Month 10-12: 250 paying customers = $7,250/month
- **Year 1 Total:** ~$42,000

**Aggressive (Year 1):**
- Month 1-3: 50 paying customers = $1,450/month
- Month 4-6: 150 paying customers = $4,350/month
- Month 7-9: 300 paying customers = $8,700/month
- Month 10-12: 500 paying customers = $14,500/month
- **Year 1 Total:** ~$85,000
